# welcome

Coinbase Onramp makes it easy for developers to enable their users to move money seamlessly between fiat and crypto. Users can onramp using a card payment method (e.g. Apple Pay, Debit Card) or by logging in with their Coinbase account. The Onramp product can be integrated in two ways:

-   **Coinbase-hosted Onramp**: A fully hosted onramp application that you can redirect users to.
-   **Headless Onramp**: Allows you to embed the Onramp flow in your application.
