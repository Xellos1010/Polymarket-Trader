Advanced Trade APIs

**Coinbase Business** uses the to support programmatic trading and order management with a [REST API](https://developer.chrome.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/rest-api) and [WebSocket protocol](https://developer.chrome.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/websocket/websocket-overview) for real-time market data. **Coinbase Advanced Trade SDKs**
