# welcome

Welcome to [Coinbase Derivatives Exchange](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives) (CDE) documentation. CDE provides REST, FIX, SBE, and UDP APIs:

-   [REST](https://developer.chrome.com/api-reference/derivatives-api/rest-api/introduction) (REST API).
-   [FIX](https://developer.chrome.com/derivatives/fix/overview) (FIX4.4 with tag-value encoding).
-   [SBE](https://developer.chrome.com/derivatives/sbe/overview) (Simple Binary Encoding).
-   [UDP](https://developer.chrome.com/derivatives/udp/overview) (User Datagram Protocol) multicast market data.

The guide is not intended to serve as a full-fledged specification of the FIX protocol. Refer to the official FIX specifications at [https://www.fixtrading.org](https://www.fixtrading.org/online-specification/) for additional details.
