# delegation flow

1.  Go to [Polygon Wallet](https://wallet-dev.polygon.technology/staking/).
2.  Connect Polygon Wallet to your self-custody wallet: a. Click **Connect to a Wallet** (top right). b. Select your self-custody wallet from the list (Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, etc) and click **Connect**. c. When prompted, sign again and confirm the connection.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/polygon-01-select-wallet.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=c20d2f46ebc9e48a77159fc0f33b1a5e)

3.  Select [Polygon Staking](https://wallet.polygon.technology/).

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/polygon-02-select-staking.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=e90c13aa5e64c8dbbdd4f6fd7f7ac920)

4.  Click **All Validators** (top left) to see a list of validators you can delegate to. Search for the “Coinbase” validator.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/polygon-03-all-validators.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=9d46c1a3d286ad54aae77e9a8d85c112)

5.  Select the Coinbase validator, and click **Delegate**.
6.  Enter the amount of MATIC you wish to delegate and click **Continue**. This step approves the delegation.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/polygon-04-enter-matic-amount.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=fb9fbc419b90b77853dddec73775a118)

7.  Sign a second transaction to actually delegate your MATIC to Coinbase validator or your chosen validator.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/polygon-05-sign-second-transaction.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=30b13b75344d1ba1425906221f4ffd7c)
