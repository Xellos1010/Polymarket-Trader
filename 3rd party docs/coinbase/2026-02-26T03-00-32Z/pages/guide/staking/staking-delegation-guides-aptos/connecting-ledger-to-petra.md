# connecting ledger to petra

This page explains how to connect Ledger Live to your Petra Wallet. Ledger Live is a mobile and desktop application that works in tandem with a Ledger hardware device to manage digital assets held in cold storage.

## Step 1. Install

This section explains how to install the Aptos app on your Ledger Live. It assumes that you have Ledger Live installed.

1.  Open [Ledger Live](https://www.ledger.com/ledger-live).

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-live.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=e6b13dfb5c695ae863faa24ede34a865)

2.  Connect your Ledger to your computer and unlock it with your PIN.
3.  Navigate to **My Ledger**, search for Aptos in the App catalog and install it.

**Result**: Ledger Live should display Aptos as installed.

## Step 2. Connect

This section explains how to connect Ledger Live to Petra. It assumes that you have [created and funded](https://developer.chrome.com/staking/staking-delegation-guides/aptos/staking-with-petra-wallet) a Petra Aptos Wallet.

1.  Open your Petra Wallet and click the avatar at the top right.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-01-avatar.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=85113b9cdd583991f47e7d7d44fd8f07)

2.  Click **Add Account**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-02-add-account.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=bc6f954b9a40197bc463ea60f55fce94)

3.  Click **Import from Ledger**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-03-import-from-ledger.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=3551ad1ef39f621dc9b8e9be5814a06f)

4.  Click **Pair**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-04-pair.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=445b533f831e3dae46ffeac5231590fc)

5.  Select your Ledger device and click **Connect**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-05-connect.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=9ef272f2430e36ceb007a7b84a58e89c)

6.  Select an account address then click **Add account**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-06-select-address.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=b027dd6491329d81df51d45b6be808a7)

7.  Click Settings, select your new account, and rename it to something meaningful.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/aptos-ledger-07-rename-account.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=72418cd00b0811fd53d374bcafcb4722)

8.  See [Step 2. Connect and Stake](https://developer.chrome.com/staking/staking-delegation-guides/aptos/staking-with-petra-wallet#step-2-connect-and-stake) to stake APT with your Petra Wallet.
