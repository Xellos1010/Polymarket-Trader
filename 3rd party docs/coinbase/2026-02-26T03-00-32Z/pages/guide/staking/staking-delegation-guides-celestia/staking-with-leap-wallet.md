# staking with leap wallet

## Step 1. Create and Fund

1.  Download the [Leap Wallet](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/leap-cosmos-wallet/fcfcfllfndlomdhbehjjcoimbgofdncg) extension and navigate to the extension on your browser.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-extension.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=c52d698d243578cc48c6a3c466243692)

2.  Click **Create new wallet** or **Import an existing wallet**.
3.  Securely store your recovery phrase and create a password.
4.  Fund the newly created address with TIA.

## Step 2. Connect to Celestia network

1.  Connect to Celestia by clicking on the chain dropdown in the top right corner.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-connect.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=0bb7b50d5d230b7ea9ad50fbf32b6118)

2.  Search for and click on Celestia.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-select-chain.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=afa903603970d8d7190f73e7650cc507)

## Step 3. Delegate to Coinbase Validator

1.  Click on **Stake** tab on the bottom of the extension.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-stake-tab.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=542b6550e6cfdc8ec47d98849e43f82c)

2.  Then click the **Stake** button.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-stake-tab.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=542b6550e6cfdc8ec47d98849e43f82c)

3.  Search for the Coinbase validator and click on it.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-stake.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=de06115b30a276c33f902f636644bfdf)

4.  Enter in your desired amount of TIA to stake, click **Review**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-leap-stake-tia.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=d50e12aab4d50f4e9f0a80b7aa4e6856)

5.  If all looks correct, complete and confirm the transaction. Once completed your funds will be delegated.
