# staking with keplr wallet

## Step 1. Create Wallet and Fund

1.  Download the [Keplr Wallet](https://www.keplr.app/) extension and navigate to the extension on your browser.
2.  Click **Create new wallet** or **Import an existing wallet**.
3.  Securely store your recovery phrase and create a password.
4.  Fund the newly created address with TIA.

## Step 2. Delegate to Coinbase Validator

1.  Navigate to the [Keplr Dashboard](https://wallet.keplr.app/) by clicking **Manage Portfolio in Keplr Dashboard** from the Keplr extension.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-extension.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=1fd4bdc03432f937d2638265bac1dc1d)

2.  Click on the **Staking** tab. If you have TIA, you will see the Celestia Staking option. Click on the **Stake** button.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-stake.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=55b18de6bb227b41d558419f066c3d8c)

3.  On the Celestia page, click the **Stake** button.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-stake-celestia.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=dbeebfd970e29a2788b93935b33af6ea)

4.  Search for the Coinbase validator and select it.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-search-validator.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=35a79cd9ae64dcd1782a05fa99aa80d8)

5.  Enter in your desired amount of TIA to stake, and click **Stake**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-stake-to-coinbase-cloud.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=c9bfb93daa4d095e469d3f7f452cd6de)

6.  If all looks correct, complete and confirm the transaction. Once completed your funds will be delegated.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/celestia-keplr-stake-approve.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=78de6c02d62c2d35b0c63a9ba1ec2f7f)
