# axelar delegation flow

1.  Go to your Keplr Wallet and log into your account.
2.  Under Chains, select **Axelar**. (On the dashboard, this appears on the left-hand side. In the browser extension, this appears at top.)

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/XD49nliTOz77YuSk/staking/images/axelar-keplr-chains.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XD49nliTOz77YuSk&q=85&s=20f37b26e40eba109b075a0cf8c3c31f)

3.  Ensure your wallet has the number of Axelar tokens you wish to delegate, plus additional AXL to cover transaction fees.
4.  Find the **Coinbase** validator from the list of validators and select **Manage** to begin the Delegation process.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/axelar-keplr-coinbase-cloud.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=0ba70afa4635f37768e748925984eb53)

5.  Select **Delegate** on the pop-up.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/axelar-keplr-delegate.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=a5478f773a057a2819a26c8889591d66)

6.  Enter the amount of AXL tokens you would like to delegate and click **Delegate**. Note the [unbonding period](https://developer.chrome.com/staking/staking-delegation-guides/axelar/axelar-delegation-guide) before your AXL becomes liquid again.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/axelar-keplr-staking-lock.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=861817b39b0b13530a03fff5b05da403)

7.  Select **Approve** in the confirmation popup (For this step, you need some AXL tokens in your unstaked balance to pay for transaction fees.) **Result**: Once the transaction is confirmed, you should see your staked AXL tokens listed in “My Validators” in the “Staking” section on Keplr.
