# staking with sui wallet

## Step 1: Setup and Fund Sui Wallet

1.  Download [Sui Wallet browser extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/sui-wallet/opcgpfmipidbgpenhmajoajpbobppdil) and navigate to the extension.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/sui-browser-extension.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=3531692d566f5608c1fa26bb2425be05)

2.  Create a new wallet.
3.  Create a password and write down your recovery phrase.
4.  Fund your newly created address with desired SUI.

## Step 2: Delegate to Coinbase Validator

1.  After creating and opening your Sui Wallet, click on **Stake & Earn SUI**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/-GFospv_XOADGRLp/staking/images/sui-sui-wallet-01-stake-n-earn.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-GFospv_XOADGRLp&q=85&s=9b88c4637daa164d13bfe4cb8db96162)

2.  Navigate to [Coinbase validator](https://suiexplorer.com/validator/0x0350925241ae8d4083fe4b1104e14dd28d2ffeced9d7575551c50c41b99995fb) by scrolling down. (Sorting by name in descending order can help.)

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/-GFospv_XOADGRLp/staking/images/sui-sui-wallet-02-find-validator.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-GFospv_XOADGRLp&q=85&s=6ce961ccfd72812d1b9b5ab65e990ce1)

3.  Enter in your desired amount, and press **Stake Now**.

*APY in product may vary.* ![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/-GFospv_XOADGRLp/staking/images/sui-sui-wallet-03-enter-amount.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-GFospv_XOADGRLp&q=85&s=bddb05dc7fe5a7cd9484a49ce552acb7)

4.  Confirm the transaction. Once completed your funds should be delegated.
