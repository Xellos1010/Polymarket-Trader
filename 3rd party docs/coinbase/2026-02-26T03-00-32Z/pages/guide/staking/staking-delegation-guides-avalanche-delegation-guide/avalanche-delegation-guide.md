# avalanche delegation guide

[Avalanche](https://www.avax.network/) is a scalable smart contracts platform for decentralized applications, with a focus on offering a high transaction throughput, near-instant finality, and low fees. Avalanche supports the deployment of Ethereum-compatible apps on its proof of stake blockchain, in addition to supporting the launch of bespoke Layer 1 (L1) and Layer 2 (L2) application-specific blockchains (subnets).
