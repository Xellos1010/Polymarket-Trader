# delegating with eigenlayer

## Step 1. Restaking on EigenLayer

1.  Navigate to the EigenLayer App and click on the **Restake** tab.

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/eigenlayer-restake.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=f75b02dc037b438e0961fa2f5e758272)

2.  Click on your relevant asset (i.e. ETH)
3.  Select the **Amount** and click **deposit**. If all looks correct, confirm the transaction through your wallet

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/eigenlayer-restake-deposit.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=a02f1fb543db2694d2762ca59760a01b)

4.  When navigating back to the **Restake** tab you should now see the total amount that was restaked. Over time, you will see EigenLayer Points earned from this restaking activity

## Step 2. Delegate to the Coinbase Operator

1.  Navigate to the [EigenLayer Operator Tab](https://app.eigenlayer.xyz/operator)

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/eigenlayer-operator.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=ae61b1b677b60e889a1d9759efda64a4)

2.  Search for the Coinbase Operator by searching the name, then click on its Node Operator tile
3.  Click on **Delegate** to initiate the transaction

![](https://mintcdn.com/coinbase-prod/w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL/staking/images/eigenlayer-operator-deposit.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=w-ScnEN6FOiONlpL&q=85&s=2c22df515623d3cb02aa754c136ebfd6)

4.  Confirm the transaction on your wallet
