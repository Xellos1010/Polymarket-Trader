Staking with Keplr

The Keplr wallet is an open-source web-based wallet for the Cosmos ecosystem, including all IBC-enabled Tendermint chains. It is accessible via the [Keplr dashboard](https://wallet.keplr.app/#/dashboard) and as a [Google Chrome browser extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/keplr/dmkamcknogkgcdfhhbddcghachkejeap). If you do not have any ATOM stored on your Keplr wallet, start at [1: Set up and fund Keplr wallet with ATOM](https://developer.chrome.com/staking/staking-delegation-guides/cosmos/staking-with-keplr/set-up-and-fund-staking-keplr) If you already have OSMO stored on your Keplr wallet or in the Osmosis UI, proceed to [2: Stake ATOM to with Keplr](https://developer.chrome.com/staking/staking-delegation-guides/cosmos/staking-with-keplr/stake-atom-to-with-keplr).
